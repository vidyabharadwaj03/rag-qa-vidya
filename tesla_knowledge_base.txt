Tesla, Inc. was founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning.
Ian Wright, Elon Musk, and J.B. Straubel joined Tesla during its early formation and development.
Tesla's mission is to accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy.
Tesla develops electric vehicles, energy storage products, solar energy products, and charging services.
The Tesla Roadster was the company's first production vehicle and was based on the Lotus Elise platform.
The Tesla Model S is a battery-electric luxury sedan.
The Tesla Model 3 is a battery-electric sedan designed for a broader market.
The Tesla Model X is a battery-electric sport utility vehicle with distinctive falcon-wing doors.
The Tesla Model Y is a battery-electric compact SUV.
Tesla vehicles use rechargeable lithium-ion battery packs to store electrical energy.
Tesla's Supercharger network provides fast charging for compatible Tesla vehicles.
Tesla Energy produces products such as the Powerwall home battery and Megapack grid battery.
Tesla headquarters are located in Austin, Texas.
Tesla's stock ticker symbol is TSLA on the Nasdaq stock exchange.
